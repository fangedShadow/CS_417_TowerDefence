


Tower Defence
A Vision Design Document




By: Jackson Drape, Bhavpreet Singh, Billy Lu





The player takes on the role of a military commander tasked with defending a strategic base against increasingly difficult waves of enemy forces. At the beginning of the game, the player chooses one of three factions: Army, Navy, or Air Force.
Each faction offers unique towers, buildings, and abilities. As the player survives more waves, they earn resources, strengthen their defenses, and unlock additional factions. The ultimate goal is to strategically combine the strengths of all three factions to protect the base against increasingly powerful enemy attacks.
The game takes place on a modern military battlefield featuring a single map with land, water, and air combat zones. Enemy forces approach the player's base through designated routes, requiring the player to strategically place defensive structures to protect the base.
The battlefield is presented in virtual reality, allowing players to interact with their defenses, manage resources, and observe combat from an immersive command perspective.

Each team member is responsible for developing one of the three military factions. Every faction will have its own unique versions of four tower categories: Attack Towers, Defense Towers, Support Towers, and Buildings.

2. Bhavpreet Singh: Responsible for designing and creating Navy-themed prefabs for Attack Towers, Defense Towers, Support Towers, and Buildings. Each structure will have unique abilities, statistics, and upgrades focused on naval warfare, long-range attacks, and battlefield support.

Each faction will contain its own versions of the following four categories:
- Attack Towers: Structures primarily responsible for damaging and eliminating enemy units. Each attack tower will have different attributes, including damage, attack speed, range, and targeting capabilities.
- Defense Towers: Structures designed to protect the base and nearby towers by absorbing damage, providing shields, creating defensive barriers, or increasing the durability of nearby structures.
- Support Towers: Structures that provide additional advantages to other towers or disadvantages to enemies, such as increasing attack damage, improving firing speed, extending range, or slowing enemy movement.
- Buildings: Larger strategic structures that provide special abilities or economic benefits, such as generating money, deploying additional units, or activating powerful attacks.
The core loop focuses on the interactions that players perform repeatedly throughout the game: constructing towers, defending against enemies, and managing resources.
1. Base Introduction and Faction Selection
The game begins with the player observing the battlefield and their base. An introductory popup explains the objective of defending the base and introduces the basic VR controls. The player chooses one of three starting factions: Army, Navy, or Sky. Only the selected faction's towers are initially available.
2. Buying and Building Towers
The player opens a VR construction menu to view available towers and their costs. Players can select towers, preview their placement on the battlefield, and construct them in valid locations using VR controllers. Each faction provides four tower categories with unique functions. The player begins by learning how to place an Attack Tower before being introduced to Defense Towers, Support Towers, and Buildings.
3. Enemy Waves and Combat
Enemies spawn in waves and travel along designated routes toward the player's base. Towers automatically detect and attack enemies within their range based on their targeting capabilities. Enemies have different health, speed, damage, and movement types, including ground, water, and air units. The player must strategically select and position towers to counter these threats.
4. Money, Experience, and Tower Management
Defeating enemies rewards players with money and experience points (EXP). Money is used to purchase new towers, upgrade existing structures, and repair damaged defenses. EXP is used to unlock new structures and upgrades .Players can select existing towers through VR interactions to view their stats and perform actions such as upgrading, repairing, or selling. Selling a tower returns a portion of its original investment.
5. Base Protection
Enemies that successfully reach the base reduce its health. Players must continuously build, upgrade, and manage defenses to prevent the base from being destroyed. 
The core gameplay cycle repeats as players defeat waves, earn resources, improve defenses, and prepare for stronger enemies.
The meta loop introduces new challenges, upgrades, and strategic opportunities as the player progresses through the game.
1. Tower Upgrades and Unlocks
As players earn resources and experience, they gain access to stronger towers and additional upgrades. Each tower has attributes such as damage, firing rate, range, durability, and special abilities. Upgrading towers improves these statistics and may unlock new capabilities. Different towers have distinct advantages and weaknesses, encouraging players to experiment with different strategies.
2. Faction Unlocks
Players begin with only one of the three factions available.
1. Wave 10: The player chooses one of the two remaining factions to unlock, gaining access to its unique towers and buildings.
2. Wave 20: The final faction becomes available, allowing the player to combine towers and abilities from all three factions.
This progression gradually introduces new gameplay mechanics and encourages players to develop more complex defensive strategies.
3. Enemy Types and Increasing Difficulty
As the player survives more waves, new enemy types and behaviors are introduced. Enemy units will vary in health, speed, damage, armor, and movement type. Examples include fast-moving enemies, heavily armored units, flying enemies, naval enemies, and powerful boss units. Later waves introduce more enemies, stronger units, and combinations of different enemy types, requiring players to adapt their defensive strategies. 
4. Unique Faction Mechanics
Each faction provides different strategic strengths and special abilities.
1. Army: Focuses on ground defenses, fortifications, heavy artillery, and durability.
2. Navy: Focuses on naval battle, naval defence, bombardment, and battlefield coverage.
3. Air Force: Focuses on aerial combat, rapid attacks, airstrikes, and targeting.
As additional factions become available, players can combine their strengths to develop more effective defensive strategies
5. Strategic Buildings and Special Abilities
Each faction includes unique Buildings that offer abilities beyond standard tower combat. These may include resource-generating facilities, command centers, unit deployment structures, and powerful weapons with cooldowns. Buildings provide additional strategic choices, requiring players to balance immediate defensive needs against longer-term investments.
6. Achievements and Milestones
Players can earn achievements by completing specific objectives during gameplay. Examples include surviving a certain number of waves, defeating a large number of enemies, constructing specific towers, fully upgrading structures, or successfully using abilities from multiple factions. Achievements encourage experimentation and reward players for reaching important milestones.
7. Endgame and Replayability
Once all three factions have been unlocked, players can combine their towers and abilities to survive increasingly difficult waves. The game can feature a final challenge wave or an endless survival mode in which players attempt to achieve the highest possible wave and score. Players can replay the game using a different starting faction or alternative tower combinations to explore different strategies.
We plan to make a Tower defense game where players take on the role of a military commander defending a strategic base against increasingly challenging waves of enemies. Players begin by selecting one of three factions: Army, Navy, or Sky, each offering unique Attack Towers, Defense Towers, Support Towers, and Buildings. Using interactive VR controls, players construct, upgrade, repair, and manage defensive structures while earning money and experience from defeating enemies. As they progress through the game, players unlock additional factions, encounter new enemy types, and gain access to stronger towers and special abilities. By combining the strengths of all three factions, players must develop effective defensive strategies to protect their base and survive increasingly difficult waves. The game aims to deliver an immersive and engaging tower defense experience that combines strategic decision-making, hands-on VR interactions, resource management, and rewarding progression.
