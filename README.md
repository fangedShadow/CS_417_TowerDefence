# VR Tower Defence

A virtual reality tower defence game where players act as a military commander
protecting a base from increasingly difficult enemy waves. Players build and
upgrade Army, Navy, and Air Force defenses, earn resources, and unlock additional
factions to combine their strengths across land, water, and air combat.

## Quit control

Open the command panel and press **Quit**, then **Quit game?** within five
seconds to exit. Waiting or closing the panel cancels confirmation. In the
Unity Editor, confirming stops Play Mode; in a build, it exits the application.
The control is saved in `GamePanel.prefab` and has no faction or combat dependencies.
